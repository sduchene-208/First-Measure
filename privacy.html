Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 31, 2026

What this policy covers

This policy explains how First Measure collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through this website and related service processes.

Information we collect

When you contact First Measure or submit the portfolio intake form, we may collect information such as your name, email address, professional background, résumé or CV, portfolio preferences, project details, and other information you choose to provide.

We may also receive basic technical information associated with a website visit, such as browser or device information, where provided by the website or service providers we use.

How we use information

We use submitted information to evaluate a portfolio project, communicate with you, prepare portfolio content and designs, deliver services, and provide support related to your project.

We do not sell the personal information you submit to us.

Third-party services

Our intake process uses third-party services, including Tally and connected Google services, to receive and organize submissions and related notifications. Information submitted through those services may also be subject to their own privacy policies and terms.

Résumés and professional materials

Please provide only information you are comfortable sharing for the purpose of evaluating and producing your portfolio. Do not submit Social Security numbers, financial account credentials, passwords, or other highly sensitive information through the portfolio intake form.

Retention and deletion

We retain client information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, or support legitimate business needs. You may contact us to request deletion of information that is not required to be retained.

Security

We take reasonable steps to limit access to client information and use appropriate safeguards for the systems we control. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Updates

We may update this policy as the business, technology, or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed through the contact method provided on the First Measure website.

This policy is a business draft and is not legal advice. Review it with a qualified attorney for your specific business and state requirements before relying on it as a final legal document.
