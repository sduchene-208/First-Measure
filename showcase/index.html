01WHO IT’S FOR

A fuller picture of who you are—and where you’re going.

Showcase is for students and young people whose experiences do not fit neatly into an application, résumé, profile, or activity list.

It can support college and scholarship applications, athletics and recruiting, performing arts, Greek-life preparation, internships, leadership, service, and long-term achievement portfolios.

A Showcase portfolio supplements required application and recruiting systems. It does not replace them or promise an outcome.

03A LIVING PORTFOLIO

Start before the application. Keep building from there.

A Showcase can begin when the story is still taking shape. Through First Measure, it can be updated as new experiences and goals emerge.

  1. NOWBegin with what matters today

    Current interests, activities, evidence, and goals.

  2. NEXTAdd the next chapter

    A season, school year, project, performance, or milestone.

  3. OVER TIMEBuild a record of growth

    New achievements, media, leadership, service, academics, and future goals.

04HOW IT WORKS

Thoughtful from the first conversation to every future chapter.

First Measure develops the story, presentation, and supporting-media approach with the student and family.

  1. 01
    Share the story

    Provide available evidence, media, achievements, and goals through the appropriate intake.

  2. 02
    Find what matters

    We identify the strongest themes, evidence, and opportunities for presentation.

  3. 03

    We create the portfolio direction, content, visual identity, and media strategy.

  4. 04

  5. 05

  6. 06
    Keep it current

    Return to First Measure when achievements and goals evolve.

EVIDENCE WITH INTEGRITY

Real experiences. Carefully presented.

Showcase is built from accurate, supportable information. Academic results, awards, statistics, rankings, offers, acceptances, affiliations, roles, activities, performances, projects, and outcomes are never invented or overstated.

PRIVACY BY INTENTION

Share what helps—not everything collected.

Public content is selected intentionally. Home addresses, birth dates, private school records, personal schedules, medical information, travel schedules, and sensitive contact details do not belong in a public Showcase.
